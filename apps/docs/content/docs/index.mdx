In a normal prediction market the loser loses everything, which keeps most people out. Staked NEAR earns about 4.5% a year anyway. Pearn makes that yield the stake.

You bet **stNEAR** (Meta Pool's liquid-staked NEAR) on YES or NO. When the market settles, everyone gets their principal's NEAR value back, and the winning side splits the staking yield the whole market earned, plus anything sponsors added.

## What makes it NEAR

- Contracts are Solidity on **Aurora**, NEAR's EVM.
- The yield is **NEAR staking**: stNEAR's NEAR value rises every epoch.
- The rate comes **from NEAR itself**: `StNearRateOracle` calls `get_st_near_price` on the Meta Pool contract through Aurora's cross-contract-call precompile and receives the answer in a Solidity callback. There is no off-chain feed.
- Everything was deployed, and the keeper runs, **without Aurora ETH**: transactions go to the Aurora engine's `submit` method from a NEAR account.

## Status

Live on Aurora testnet and verified on Blockscout ([contracts](/docs/contracts/)). There is no owner, no upgrade path, and no admin access to funds.
