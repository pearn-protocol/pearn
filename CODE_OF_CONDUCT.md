# Code of conduct

Pearn follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticise ideas and not people, and keep harassment, personal attacks and other people's private information out of every Pearn space (issues, pull requests, discussions and social channels).

**Reporting.** Contact the maintainers privately through a [private vulnerability report](https://github.com/pearn-protocol/pearn/security/advisories/new) (it reaches only the maintainers, so it works for conduct reports too) or a direct message to a maintainer. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content and restrict participation as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
